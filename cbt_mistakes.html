A solid cylinder of mass $M$ and radius $R$ is placed on a rough horizontal surface. A horizontal force $F$ is applied at a height $h$ from the center of mass. For pure rolling without slipping, determine the ratio $h/R$:
✓ Verified Solution & Autopsy Notes:
Torque about contact point: $\tau = F(R + h) = I_{\text{contact}} \alpha$. For pure rolling, $I_{\text{contact}} = \frac{3}{2}MR^2$. Linear acceleration $a = R \alpha \implies h = R/2 \implies h/R = 0.5$.
