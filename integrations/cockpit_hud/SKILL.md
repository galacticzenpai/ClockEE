# Integrated Floating Cockpit HUD Overlay for ClockEE

Reel: #35 (https://www.instagram.com/p/Dfgr-20D5Hw/)
Package: `integrations/cockpit_hud/`

## Capabilities
- Injects a non-intrusive floating indicator in the lower-left corner of all pages.
- Displays live pulse indicating IndexedDB storage health, online connectivity, and timer ticks.
- Clicking badge toggles the full Diagnostics Console (Reel #24).
