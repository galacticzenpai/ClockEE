## 3. Defect Breakdown & Reproduction

### DEFECT-1 (Severity: P1 — Visual Collision & Accessibility Occlusion)
- **Title**: Top Navbar Element Collision between `CBT Exam Suite ▼` and `Palette ▼`
- **Location**: Top Navigation Bar (`#clockee-nav`, desktop viewport 1280x800)
- **Observed Behavior**:
  - `CBT Exam Suite ▼` (bounding box $x=751.9\text{px}$ to $917.2\text{px}$) overlaps with the `Palette ▼` dropdown button ($x=862.6\text{px}$ to $963.0\text{px}$).
  - Results in a **$54.6\text{px}$ horizontal collision**. The gear icon and text visually clip into the palette pill, and the browser accessibility tree marks the CBT Exam Suite link as `[occluded]`.
- **Root Cause**:
  - Unconstrained navigation items inside `#clockee-nav` with insufficient responsive container flexing or lack of `min-width` / `overflow: hidden` rules.
- **Remediation**:
  - Wrap navigation items with proper flex shrink/gap constraints or collapse secondary utility buttons into the quick command menu on viewports $\le 1280\text{px}$.

---

### DEFECT-2 (Severity: P1 — Interactive Occlusion & Hitbox Blocking)
- **Title**: Floating Controls Dock Occludes Maths Practice Quick Log Submission Controls
- **Location**: Bottom Right Viewport (`#clockee-floating-dock` / `.quick-log-card:nth-child(3)`)
- **Observed Behavior**:
  - The floating system dock (`Palette Ctrl+K` and `AI Tutor` pills) fixed at the bottom right corner sits directly on top of the Maths Practice card in the Quick Log grid.
  - The `Custom` numeric input and `Log` CTA button are partially or fully occluded by the floating pills, causing misclicks and preventing students from logging mathematics questions without zooming or scrolling artificially.
- **Root Cause**:
  - Insufficient bottom padding (`pb-24` or margin) on the main content container to account for `fixed bottom-4 right-4` floating elements.
- **Remediation**:
  - Add `padding-bottom: 80px` (`pb-20` / `pb-24`) to the main content wrapper `#clockee-main-content` to ensure full clearance above the floating dock.

---

### DEFECT-3 (Severity: P2 — Ergonomic & Touch Target Size Violation)
- **Title**: Sub-44px Touch Targets Across Interactive Controls and Pagination
- **Location**: Master Navigation links, Quick Log stepper buttons, Heatmap pagination
- **Observed Behavior**:
  - 59 of 89 interactive controls on desktop and mobile fall below the Apple HIG / Fitts's Law standard of $44 \times 44\text{px}$:
    * Quick Log stepper buttons (`+1`, `+5`, `-1`): height $= 32.0\text{px}$ (deficit: $-12\text{px}$).
    * Quick Log `Custom` input & `Log` button: height $= 28.0\text{px}$ (deficit: $-16\text{px}$).
    * Heatmap pagination arrows (`◀`, `▶`): $24 \times 24\text{px}$ (deficit: $-20\text{px}$).
    * Navigation links: height $= 33.5\text{px}$.
- **Remediation**:
  - Apply `min-h-[44px]` (or invisible pseudo-element padding `::after` with minimum 44px hit bounds) to stepper buttons, inputs, and pagination arrows.

---

### DEFECT-4 (Severity: P2 — Color Contrast Non-Conformance)
- **Title**: Secondary Text Color Contrast Fails WCAG 2.1 AA Normal Text Requirement ($< 4.5:1$)
- **Location**: Sub-labels, descriptive cards, and micro-badges
- **Observed Behavior**:
  - `text-slate-500` (`#64748b`) against Deep Obsidian canvas (`#08090c`) yields **$4.02:1$** contrast.
  - `text-slate-500` against elevated card background (`#0d1117`) yields **$3.86:1$** contrast.
  - Both fail the WCAG 2.1 AA requirement of $\ge 4.5:1$ for normal text.
- **Remediation**:
  - Upgrade secondary text color token from `text-slate-500` (`#64748b`) to `text-slate-400` (`#94a3b8`, contrast **$7.78:1$**) or `#8899ac` (contrast $\ge 5.2:1$).
